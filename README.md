<summary><b>① MoneyMic: AI spending alignment agent</b> · <i>The Ken's Great Rewiring 2026, Round 3 (semifinals)</i></summary>

<br>

- **Problem:** People know where their money went. They still overspend.
- **The call:** 26 user interviews showed people already building their own workarounds: spending caps, handing money to someone else, converting to cash. I scrapped my expense-tracker idea and pivoted to spending *alignment*.
- **Decision I'm proudest of:** the agent isn't rewarded for acting more often. Staying silent is a valid output.
- **Built:** a 12-state agent lifecycle, 17 capabilities mapped across 3 partner rails, a North Star metric (deviation from intended spend), and a working MVP with real speech-to-text.

💻 [Repo](https://github.com/kaunnikhil/MoneyMic) · 🎥 [Demo video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bnpaBHH6bN3JnPSLHlldAkCiunzP6c1T/view) · 📄 [PRD](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jxZAPB_5h6b6iROyKeIRTuBflKlfUej_rp-6Ws9gvd0/edit) · 🎨 [Figma flows](https://www.figma.com/design/pQNVPseKV9gXbPyZwtMUug/MoneyMic?node-id=27-4792&t=s49HbKz0RmQUbr4S-0) · 🌐 [Landing page](https://moneymic.lovable.app/) · 📁 [Project folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1nsEMfrwmYOYz77FfHA9sRga59dJQrf4P4)

<summary><b>② StratIQ: AI analyst for KPI investigations</b> · <i>Built for the Accenture Innovation Challenge</i></summary>

<br>

- **Problem:** Dashboards tell you *that* a KPI dropped. They never tell you *why*.
- **The call:** Treat it as an analyst workflow for PMs: validate the anomaly, rank competing hypotheses, recommend next actions.

## 🛠️ Sheet 3: Standard Operating Procedure

1. **Is it a real problem, or just *my* problem?** What bothers me isn't automatically what bothers everyone. I test that before I build.
2. **User-centric, not user-obedient.** What people ask for and what a product can sustainably deliver aren't always the same thing. I build in the overlap.
3. **Metric before feature.** If I can't say how I'll know it worked, it isn't ready to build.
4. **Break it myself, first.** I assume everything goes wrong in practice and map every breakpoint before anyone else finds it. (MoneyMic has a happy-flow PDF *and* an unhappy-flow PDF.)
5. **Decide what *not* to build.** Restraint is a feature.
6. **Learn what I don't know.** A missing skill is a to-do item, not a blocker.
